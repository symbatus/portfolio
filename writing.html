Writing

Two peer-reviewed papers published during my undergraduate studies.

Analysis of Media Manipulation Strategies During Military Conflicts

Aidarbekov, A., Ussupova, S., & Baizhuminova, B. (2024). Academics and Science Reviews Materials, No. 6. Read the paper

The peer-reviewed version of the research behind my undergraduate thesis. It examines how Russian and Ukrainian news coverage of the war used different strategies to frame the conflict, including discrediting, disinformation, and different ways of presenting information.

My role: Second author. I developed the qualitative frame matrix, coded the media sample, and conducted the main analysis.

The Features of the TCP/IP Layers

Kantayeva, A., & Ussupova, S. (2022). Modern Research and Development, 55–58. Read the paper

An early technical paper on the four TCP/IP layers and their data communication functions.

My role: Co-author.
