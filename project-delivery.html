Delivery Shift

Game jam prototype

Spark Game Jam 2026 · 3 days · Playable in browser

A cozy sci-fi platformer about UNIT-7, a cheaply built delivery robot working the night shift at an orbital space hotel. Deliver the package before your battery runs out. When it runs out, you explode, and the next bot in the queue is sent to finish the same job.

The game

UNIT-7 works for STARPOST Delivery Service aboard Voyager-1, a sprawling multi-level space hotel. The hotel is large, the routes are awkward, and the robot is built cheap and powered weak. Running out of battery is not a fail state so much as a shift change: the bot explodes, a replacement is dispatched, and the delivery continues.

Progress persists even though the robot does not. Along the way you scavenge modules that change what routes are available — a tire module for high-friction tracks, a propeller for delivery points you cannot reach on wheels. The joke and the mechanic are the same thing: the worker is disposable, the parcel is not.
