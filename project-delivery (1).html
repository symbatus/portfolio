Delivery Shift

Game jam prototype

Spark Game Jam 2026 · 3 days · Playable in browser

A cozy sci-fi platformer about UNIT-7, a cheaply built delivery robot working the night shift at an orbital space hotel. Deliver the package before your battery runs out. If you fail, the robot explodes and the next one is sent to finish the same job.

The game

UNIT-7 works for STARPOST Delivery Service aboard Voyager-1, a large space hotel. Its battery is weak, so running out of power is part of the job: the robot explodes, a replacement arrives, and the delivery continues.

Progress carries over between robots. Players collect modules that unlock new ways to move through the hotel, such as a tire for high-friction surfaces or a propeller for reaching higher areas.

The main idea was simple: the robot can be replaced, but the delivery cannot.

What I made

I worked on both the visual direction and game design. I created the 3D environment and designed UNIT-7 to look cheap but charming. I also developed the core mechanics and narrative around the robot's repeated shifts and replacement.
