Pharos

Mobile action-strategy game

ColdLight Games · Android · In development · 2025

A Lovecraft-inspired mobile game about a lighthouse that guides ships through the night while defending itself from creatures attracted by its light. Built with ColdLight Games, an independent team I co-founded during the Gamedev Teams Pipeline 3.0 programme.

My role
Co-founder, Lead 3D Artist, UI/UX Designer, and Game Designer. I defined the visual direction and created the lighthouse, environment, materials, lighting, and other 3D assets. I also worked on the game design, including gameplay mechanics and the overall structure of the game.

The game

The player controls a lighthouse that has to guide ships safely through the night while defending itself from creatures attracted by its light.

The main mechanic is built around this conflict: the light protects the ships, but it also attracts the enemies. During the day, players can meet rescued sailors, collect rewards, and upgrade the lighthouse before the next night.

Environment and visual direction

I focused mainly on the 3D environment and the visual style of the game — from the lighthouse and sea to the fog, materials, and lighting.

Because the game was made for mobile, everything had to remain readable on a small screen, especially during the darker scenes. I used silhouettes, lighting, and large shapes to separate the lighthouse, ships, enemies, and background without relying on small details.
