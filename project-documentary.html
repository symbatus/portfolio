About the project

This documentary explores how modern Kazakh animation shapes the cultural identity, values, and self-perception of children and young audiences in Kazakhstan. Through the daily work and insights of the animators at Ara Film Studio, the film investigates the vital role of animated storytelling in preserving language, traditions, and national heritage in an increasingly globalized media landscape. We wanted to show not just how these stories are made, but why they matter.
