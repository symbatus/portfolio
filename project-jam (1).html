Servant's Brew

Game jam prototype

RAID Game Jam 2025 · 4 days · Playable in browser

A game about a clumsy skeleton servant who brews potions and throws them to his undead lord during a battle. Built in four days at RAID Game Jam 2025, where our team placed 2nd out of 105 teams.

3D Artist — props, environment assets, and visual style.

What I made

I worked on the 3D art for the game, creating the cauldron, bottles, ingredients, brewing station, and dungeon environment. I also helped shape the overall visual style.

With only a few days to build the prototype, we focused on simple shapes and readable props that could quickly establish the setting and characters.
