About

I studied media in Kazakhstan while also learning 3D modeling and game development. Since then, I have continued working across both areas, moving between research, creative projects, and technical work.

My work now spans media research, AI-based tools, games, 3D graphics, film, and animation. I am interested in how information is presented, how stories change across different media, and how different formats can change the way we understand them.
